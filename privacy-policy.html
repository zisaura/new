This Privacy Policy explains how Zisaura ("Zisaura," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit zisaura.com (the "Site") or submit information to us through the Site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a contact or booking form, including your name, business name, work email, phone number (if provided), the service you are interested in, and any message you submit. If you book a call through our scheduling tool, that tool (Calendly) collects your name, email, and selected time slot directly, subject to its own privacy policy.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, through standard web analytics tools, for the purpose of understanding Site usage and improving the Site.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule consultation calls
  • To evaluate and provide the services you request
  • To send you information about our services, if you have engaged with us (you may opt out at any time)
  • To improve the Site and our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party tools to operate the Site and our business, which may process your information on our behalf, including: Calendly (scheduling), Stripe (payment processing), OpenPhone (business communications), and standard website hosting/analytics providers. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

4. Data Retention

We retain information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide services, maintain business records, and comply with legal or tax obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information submitted to us. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us by emailing hello@zisaura.com. You may unsubscribe from any marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link provided or by contacting us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site and Services are intended for business use by adults and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Users

Zisaura provides services to businesses located in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
